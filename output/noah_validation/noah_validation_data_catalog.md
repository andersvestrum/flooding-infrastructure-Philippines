| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gfd | Global Flood Database (GFD) | Historical event validation | 2000-2018 (used here for pre-2014 named events and overlapping 2015 events) | Raster flood extent | ~250 m (MODIS-derived) | Sample flooded raster at 250 m city-grid cell centres; union event IDs within named event windows. | Event distribution, event heatmap, source heatmap, pooled source scatter |
